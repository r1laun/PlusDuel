Privacy Policy — PlusDuel

Last updated: October 14, 2026 · App: PlusDuel — Real-time 1v1 Math Duel (package com.onrender.plusduel.twa) · Website: plusduel.onrender.com

1. What PlusDuel is

PlusDuel is a free browser-first 1v1 math duel game. You can play as a guest with a nickname, or optionally sign in to keep rating across devices. The Android app distributed via Google Play is a Trusted Web Activity (TWA) wrapper of the same website — this policy covers both.

2. Data we collect

  • Nickname & gameplay data (guest): nickname you enter (stored on your device via localStorage), match results, rating, round history. Used only to run matchmaking and leaderboards.
  • Account data (optional sign-in via Supabase): email address and authentication tokens, to link guest rating to your account across devices. Sign-in is optional.
  • Technical / analytics data: device type, OS / browser version, coarse session analytics via Google Analytics 4 (page views, session duration, anonymous client IDs / cookies), and real-time Socket.io connection identifiers required for multiplayer.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or advertising IDs. We show no third-party ads.

3. How we use data

  • Run matchmaking, private rooms, scoring and leaderboards.
  • Keep you signed in (if you choose sign-in) and restore rating.
  • Measure aggregate usage and fix bugs / improve gameplay (GA4).
  • Prevent abuse and enforce game rules.

Legal basis (EEA/UK): performance of the game service you requested, our legitimate interest in analytics / security, and your consent for optional sign-in and cookies where required.

4. Sharing

  • Supabase (authentication & account data) — processes email + auth tokens on our behalf.
  • Google Analytics 4 (analytics) — processes pseudonymous usage data and cookies.
  • Render (hosting) — hosts the website / game server; processes IP addresses and connection logs to deliver the service.

We do not sell personal data. We disclose data only when required by law.

5. Storage & retention

Guest nickname and device ID stay in your browser (localStorage) until you clear site data. Account data is kept in Supabase while your account exists. Analytics data is retained per Google Analytics settings (default 14 months, aggregate reports longer). Server match logs are in-memory / short-lived operational data.

6. Your rights & deletion

You can always play as guest without an account. To exercise access, correction, deletion, or to delete your account and linked rating/email, contact us at nuraly2907@icloud.com with the subject “PlusDuel deletion request”. Signing out does not delete server-side account data — deletion happens on request. Clearing browser site data removes local guest data immediately.

7. Children

PlusDuel is a general-audience puzzle game, not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Traffic is encrypted via HTTPS/WSS. Access tokens are transmitted only over encrypted connections. No method is 100% secure, but we apply reasonable safeguards and least-privilege access.

9. International transfers

Hosting / analytics providers (Render, Supabase, Google) may process data outside your country, including the US, under their standard contractual safeguards.

10. Contact

Data controller: PlusDuel. Contact email: nuraly2907@icloud.com. Website: https://plusduel.onrender.com/.

11. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date. Continued use after changes means acceptance.
