How to request deletion

Send an email to nuraly2907@icloud.com with the subject:

PlusDuel deletion request

Include in the email:

  1. Your registered email address (the one used to sign in)
  2. Your nickname (if you remember it)
  3. A brief confirmation: «Please delete my account and all associated data.»

We will process your request within 14 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your Supabase account (email, authentication tokens)
  • Linked rating, match history, and leaderboard entries
  • Any cached profile data on our servers

What we cannot delete

  • Anonymized match logs already aggregated for analytics (stripped of user identifiers)
  • Data already shared with analytics providers under their retention policies

Guest data (no account)

If you played as a guest without signing in, your nickname and device ID are stored only in your browser's local storage. Clear your site data to remove them instantly — no server-side record exists.
